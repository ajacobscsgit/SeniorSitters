Find the Right Care for Your Loved One

We'll help you determine whether non-medical companionship is the right fit.

SeniorSitters is a great fit if you need:

  • Companionship and friendly conversation
  • Errands and outings assistance
  • Meal reminders and light household support
  • Appointment accompaniment
  • Family relief and respite care
Request Care

You may need a higher level of care if:

  • Hands-on personal care (bathing, toileting)
  • Medication administration
  • Skilled nursing or monitoring
  • Mobility assistance (lifting/transfers)

If you're unsure, we're happy to help guide you to right resources.
