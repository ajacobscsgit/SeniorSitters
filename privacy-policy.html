Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

SeniorSitters is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, respectful experience when using our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you:

  • Request care services
  • Apply for a position with SeniorSitters
  • Contact us with questions or feedback
  • Sign up for updates or communications

Information we may collect includes:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Mailing address and service location
  • Details about your care needs or availability
  • Background information (for job applicants)
  • Any other information you choose to provide

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your requests and inquiries
  • Process care service requests and match you with appropriate sitters
  • Review job applications and contact candidates
  • Send you service updates and follow-up communications
  • Communicate updates and follow-ups
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure data storage and transmission
  • Limited access to personal information
  • Regular security reviews
  • Compliance with applicable privacy laws

However, no security system is impenetrable. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only when necessary with:

  • Service providers who assist in our operations (only as needed)
  • Legal representatives if required by law
  • Caregivers and clients, strictly for service coordination and with appropriate consent

All third parties are bound by confidentiality agreements and use your information only for the purposes specified.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request corrections
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date above.
