Why We Started

Our Shared Mission

We started SeniorSitters with a simple belief: seniors deserve to feel supported, respected, and connected without losing their independence. Families are often pulled in multiple directions, trying to balance work, life, and caregiving. At the same time, many seniors face quiet isolation, even when they’re still capable of living on their own.

SeniorSitters exists to bridge that gap. We provide reliable, non-medical companion care built on consistency, trust, and real human connection. As a locally owned service, we’re not a large corporation we’re directly involved, focused on showing up, building relationships, and giving families true peace of mind.
