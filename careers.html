Join the SeniorSitters Team

Make a meaningful impact while building a flexible and rewarding career.

Join SeniorSitters and make a meaningful difference in the lives of seniors while building a flexible, reliable career.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly conversation and meaningful companionship
  • Assist with daily routines and personal organization (non-medical support only)
  • Perform light housekeeping such as tidying, dishes, and general upkeep
  • Prepare simple meals and provide hydration and reminder support
  • Accompany clients on outings, errands, and appointments
  • Provide brief visit updates to families when requested

Pay & Compensation

SeniorSitters offers consistent, dependable pay for meaningful work supporting clients in their daily lives.

  • Starting pay: $17/hour
  • Weekly pay that is reliable and on time
  • Mileage reimbursement for approved client-related travel
  • Paid time for outings and appointments

Travel outside of approved client-related trips is not reimbursed.

Schedule & Flexibility

  • Flexible scheduling based on availability
  • Part-time roles with opportunities for consistent weekly hours
  • Ability to select preferred days and time windows

Benefits & Perks

  • Weekly pay
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Paid outings (restaurants, parks, events with clients)
  • Mileage reimbursement for approved travel
  • Supportive and respectful team environment
  • Opportunities for growth as the company expands
  • Performance-based bonuses
  • Company badge/ID provided
  • Branded apparel for outings and events

Holidays & Time Off

  • Recognition of major holidays
  • Eid Al-Fitr and Eid Al-Adha observed with 2 days off
  • Holiday shifts may include increased pay

Requirements

  • Reliable transportation
  • Valid driver’s license
  • Strong communication skills
  • Compassionate and dependable personality
  • Ability to pass a background check
  • Consistent punctuality and reliability

SeniorSitters provides non-medical companion care only. Employees are not permitted to perform medical or skilled care tasks.
